# ADHD Focus - Concentration Timer

![Timer Preview](public/preview.png)

## 🌟 [Click to Use](https://www.adhd-focus.ailula.top/)

This is a simple concentration timer website designed to help people with ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder) better focus. The project was inspired by my personal experience — discovering that ADHD focus music genuinely helps me calm down and maintain concentration.

## Features

### ⏱️ Focus Timer
- 🎯 Clean circular timer interface with intuitive mouse control
- 🌲 Calming forest background videos that loop seamlessly
- ⏸️ Pause/Resume functionality for flexible work sessions
- 🎵 Ambient background music that auto-stops when timer ends
- 🎨 Elegant visual feedback and smooth animations

### 📝 Interstitial Journaling (New!)
- 📌 Quick micro-journaling for task transitions
- ⏰ Automatic timestamping for all entries
- 💾 Local storage persistence with daily auto-reset
- 📤 Export daily journal as text file
- 🎭 Translucent panel design with backdrop blur
- 🔄 Collapsible interface (default hidden, non-intrusive)

## ✨ What is Interstitial Journaling?

Recently, I discovered a powerful productivity technique called **Interstitial Journaling** that works exceptionally well for people with ADHD. After testing it personally and finding it incredibly helpful, I've integrated it into this website.

Interstitial journaling is a micro-journaling practice where you briefly note:
- ✓ What you just completed
- → What you're about to do next

This simple practice helps:
- 🧠 Maintain focus and task awareness
- 🔄 Smooth transitions between tasks
- 📊 Track your actual work patterns throughout the day
- 🎯 Combat ADHD-related task switching and distraction
- 🔍 Build awareness of how you actually spend your time

## How to Use

### Focus Timer
1. Move your mouse to the outer ring of the timer to set your desired duration
2. Click the outer ring to start the timer
3. Move your mouse to the inner circle to pause/resume at any time
4. Background music will automatically stop when time is up

### Interstitial Journal
1. Click the **calendar icon** on the right side to open the journal panel
2. After completing a task, note down what you just finished (✓ field)
3. Write what you plan to do next (→ field)
4. Press **Enter** to move between fields, **Shift+Enter** to save the entry
5. Your entries are automatically saved and timestamped
6. Click the **download button** to export your daily journal
7. The journal automatically clears at midnight for a fresh start

> The journal panel is translucent with a beautiful backdrop blur, allowing you to see the calming background while staying organized. It's designed to be non-intrusive yet easily accessible whenever you need it.
